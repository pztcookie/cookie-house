# Cookie House · A Little San Francisco

A cozy, playable 3D San Francisco made with Cookie and friends. Available in 中文, English, and 日本語.

Open the website and start exploring: visit the cottage, Japantown, Chinatown, Golden Gate, and Fisherman’s Wharf. Ride a cable car, buy treats, arrange food-shaped toys, share tea or chowder, watch sea lions, and take a Polaroid with a date and note.

## How to play

- Choose a neighborhood at the top, then a place. Click a building to approach before entering.
- Select a friend below to follow or control them. Use arrow keys / WASD on desktop, or the touch joystick on a phone.
- Collect coins, shop, and select friends for shared activities. Wharf chowder costs 6 coins per bowl.
- Open the corner **How to play · Template** button for help and the reusable creation template.
- Choose Chinese, English, or Japanese at the top right. Sunny Scoops shop text intentionally stays in English.

Progress is stored in the current browser at this website origin, not in a shared multiplayer world. Changing browsers, devices, or domains does not automatically transfer progress. Photos can be downloaded individually. The camera is optional and only requested when you choose to use it.

## Make your own world

[Photo World Builder guide and demos](https://pztcookie.github.io/photo-world-builder/)

[Download the portable skill](https://pztcookie.github.io/photo-world-builder/photo-world-builder.zip)

Unzip the skill, ask Codex to read `photo-world-builder/SKILL.md`, then attach 1–6 photos and say:

> Use the Photo World Builder template to build my little world from these photos. The detail I most want to keep is: ____.

The guide provides the reusable template. This repository is the Cookie House example world.

## Local preview

Run `python3 -m http.server 5188 --bind 127.0.0.1` in this folder and open `http://127.0.0.1:5188/`. No npm, backend, API keys, or account are needed to play. A browser with WebGL is required.

Three.js 0.170.0 and the Sunny Scoops Chewy font are included with their licenses. Nunito and Pacifico are requested from Google Fonts, with system fallbacks. This public copy contains no raw reference photos, chat history, creator backups, or personal browser saves.

Fisherman’s Wharf geometry draws on the creator’s reference photographs and the public PIER 39 sea lion area. This is a stylized personal world, not an exact street map or an official attraction website.
